VIDEO PLAYER - FIELD HARD DISK (waves 0-3)
==========================================

A bootable 32 MB fixed disk: the os8088 kernel, the
Video Player, five videos and four benches. There are
two of these images and they differ only in the videos'
layout:

  VIDHERC.VHD   the videos made for a Hercules card
  VIDCGA.VHD    the videos made for CGA 640x200 - they
                play on a CGA and on a VGA

A video plays only on the adapter its layout was made
for (SPEC.md 98.3); anything else says "made for ...".
On a two-card machine it is the display the Video
Player's window is on that decides.

THE VIDEOS - double-click one, or open VIDEO.O88 and
use File > Open. P plays it fullscreen; Esc stops.
Silent in this wave, whatever the card.
CLOSE THE BENCH WINDOWS FIRST. Each keeps its memory
until it is closed, and the player reads ahead into
whatever is free: with the benches open it gets the
smallest ring (two 32 KB slots) and STALLS, or says
"Not enough memory to play it".

  BADAPPLE.V88   3:39, 30 fps - the long one
  THUNDERC.V88   1:16, 23.976 fps
  TRONDISC.V88   0:48, 23.976 fps
  BBBB_BW.V88    0:07, 60 fps
  BBBBCOMP.V88   0:07, 60 fps, made for CGA
                 COMPOSITE colour: in mono it is a
                 pattern of stripes, and that is expected

When a play ends the window reads "Drew N of N",
"stalls" (the reader could not keep up), "Late" (frames
drawn after their time) and the ticks it took against
the ticks it should have. Please photograph that for
each video. Zero late and zero stalls is the target.

THE BENCHES - each opens a window. Click it (or press
the key named) to run. Space / PgDn / PgUp page the
results. EACH ONE SAVES ITS RESULTS AS A .TXT FILE ON
THIS DISK when it finishes, so photographs are only a
backup: copy the .TXT files off afterwards.

  VIDBENCH.O88 -> VIDBENCH.TXT   (R)
    The decoder against the card: every frame in
    VIDBENCH.DAT drawn three ways and timed. The last
    lines give the worst frame in thousandths of a
    frame period at 30 and 23.976 fps - under 1000
    fits. Screen garbage while it runs is the frames.

  VIDDISK.O88 -> VIDDISK.TXT     (R, about 2 minutes)
    Streaming BADAPPLE.V88 off this disk: the old read
    at growing offsets, the new streaming read, the
    int 13h calls it makes, and the SILENT PLAYER'S
    CEILING - KB/s while a 30 Hz interrupt holds 0,
    25, 50 and 75% of every frame. The screen stays
    still for 25 s of that; it is working.

  VIDSND.O88 -> VIDSND.TXT       (R, about 50 s)
    The Sound Blaster, if there is one: interrupts per
    frame at 30 and 60 fps and 4-bit ADPCM, then whole
    tracks off this disk while the sound interrupt
    burns 0-75% of a frame. With no card it says so.
    The buffer is silence.

  VIDKERN.O88 -> VIDKERN.TXT     (A, about 30 s)
    Wave 2's kernel changes on real iron. The rate
    clock: the "periods/tick x10000" line should read
    close to 16478, and "BIOS 40:6C moved" should
    equal "ticks elapsed". Then the progress-box fence:
    LOOK at the screen - a small progress box shows for
    the first 2 s park and in neither of the next two.
    Its READ_SEQ rows need a special file and are
    skipped here; VIDDISK covers them.

Everything the benches write is their own .TXT; VIDKERN
writes and deletes one scratch file, VKTMP.DAT. Nothing
else on the disk is changed.
