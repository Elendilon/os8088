# Bounce Bonanza: the patches kept outside the shipped source

These are measured changes that the owner chose not to ship yet. They are kept
here for a possible re-do, in the style of docs/plans/khp1/patches/. Treat each
one as a **reference to re-apply, not a drop-in**. It applies to the commit it
names and may need adapting to a later tree.

| patch | what it is | against | measured | when to use it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `death-frames-a-one-shift.patch` | **A** (SPEC.md 130.38.10). Every squash, shatter, stretch and pop frame of the three balls is stored as ONE 160-byte entry at shift 0, not four shifts. At the draw, for a shift s of 1 - 3, `bz_eshift` (bzgame.inc, beside `bz_sprite`) moves the entry's 32 rows s pixels right into a 160-byte scratch in the ball part (`BZ_BAL_SCROFF`, after the shadow masks). The mask fills with kept pixels and the frame with 0. `bz_smsk` then points at the scratch, so the blit, the finish's dissolve and the boxes need no change. It also carries the tool side: `ent_rows` shifts as the guest does, the scratch, the checks' right-edge rule. The unicorn proof `eshift_host` in tests/bonanza_phys.py runs all 42 frame-and-shift pairs against `ent_rows` and has a red arm, `eshfill`. It also brings back the `EPAR`/`ESH` switches of bonanza_assets.py, at `EPAR = 1`, `ESH = 1`. | `bb-spike` `ed1890431` (B shipped). The patch is `git diff ed1890431 f46abe9f5` over apps/, tools/ and tests/; `git apply --check` passes there | **+113 resident image bytes** (49,870 -> 49,983: `bz_eshift` 106, the generator's one-shift store +6, `bz_sprite` +1), bss 0. **-6,240 bytes of resident-bundle RAM over B** (30,320 -> 24,080: -6,720 of entries, +480 of scratch, 160 a ball). Packed 4,992 -> 3,984. **10,943 / 14,788 / 18,633 clocks a drawn frame at shifts 1 / 2 / 3** (2.3 / 3.1 / 3.9 ms; PRICED by tracing under unicorn with `bzballchk.price`, not measured on MartyPC). It runs once a frame and only while the ball squashes or dies; never at shift 0. The look: a shard of glass's shatter or a pop shred left of the sprite's first pixel (x -2, -1) is not drawn at shifts 1 - 3, and a piece the shift pushes past the right edge is cut without its black ring - each for a frame or two of a burst. LOOKED AT on MartyPC, CGA and Hercules | a **256KB machine at finalization, with short courses** (the owner: "B+A might get us to a 256k system, barely, at the end, with only short courses and some other cuts"). Not built: **caching the last entry and shift** (about 20 bytes and 3 of bss), so a ball that dies and no longer moves shifts once rather than every frame |
