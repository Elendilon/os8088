# Eleven DOS games under the box — 2026-09-16

**Taken on `919a621`** (`claude/dos-arena-home`), under MartyPC on
`os8088_xt_vga` and `os8088_xt_vga_144` — a 4.77 MHz 8088 with a VGA. The
reference column is **IBM DOS 3.30** booted off a real floppy under the same
emulator, same disk, same machine.

This is a MEASUREMENT and true of that tree only. A later tree wants a new
file, not an edit to this one.

The software is the owner's own collection and is not in this repository. What
is reproducible from here is the METHOD: `tools/os88intmon.py` for the
`INT 21h` traffic, `Marty.disk()` for the drive, and the EXE header's
`minalloc` for what a program is asking the loader for.

## The three defects it found, in the order they bit

1. **`AH=25h` destroyed `AX`** (§96.7.1.3). `.setvec` fetched the program's
   `DS` off the banked frame with `mov ax, [bp]`. Every compiled DOS program
   installs its 8087 emulator's vectors with `mov ax, 2534h / int 21h /
   inc ax / loop`, so the second lap ran with a fabricated `AH`: `41h` DELETE
   FILE, then `00h` TERMINATE. Nineteen calls and BOLOBALL was gone.
2. **Fourteen of DOS's own vectors were `0000:0000`** (§96.5.2). Not
   *unimplemented* — a jump to address zero, and the probe IS the call.
   BOLOBALL asks `int 2Ah AH=00h` three instructions after a version check we
   answer correctly.
3. **The allocator never coalesced adjacent free blocks** (§96.9.2). Keen 2
   was granted 483 KB and later refused 420 KB with 221 KB free above it in
   three pieces.

**None of the three is visible in our own trace.** Every call was answered
correctly; only the sequence was wrong, and only the register, the vector or
the chain carries it. That is docs/DOS-DEBUGGING.md's premise restated by three
more cases: what found all three was putting the same program in front of a
real IBM DOS and aligning on (function, call site).

## What the arena decides, and what it does not

Two of the eleven want more than the windowed box has, and both refusals are
**correct**:

| | wants | windowed arena | kern_dos |
|---|---|---|---|
| TOM & JERRY | 483 KB at LOAD (`minalloc`) | 446 KB | 579 KB |
| COMMANDER KEEN 2 | grows to 420 KB at RUN | 446 KB | 579 KB |

Keen is the one to read twice. It fails in the windowed box for a real reason
and it failed in **kern_dos for a different one**, and the two look identical
from outside — the same message at the same call. The first theory, that
kern_dos was 11 KB short of DOS's 590, was **tested and refuted**: a
`KD_RAH_KEEP=0` build hands the program 588 KB and it fails at the *identical*
call. The quantity was never the problem.

## Chip 'n Dale — open

`RR.EXE` reads **834 sectors and the drive then stops dead**: `read_sectors`
identical across four samples 30 guest seconds apart, screen frozen on
*"Please wait…loading"* with `cell1.001` in the corner. The file is on the
disk. It is not slowness — Agent, which IS slow, keeps reading.

## Two things about the harness, for whoever runs this next

**`os8088_xt_vga_144` is new and the tree needed it.** Every VGA profile here
had 360 KB drives and every 720 KB/1.44 MB profile was a Hercules or a CGA, so
a program whose own documentation says `Req. EGA/VGA` and whose payload is
600 KB could be hosted on neither. The 40-cylinder trap
(docs/DOS-DEBUGGING.md, `reach`) is the other half: the 720 KB profiles here
are 40-cylinder, so a 720 KB image's top half answers an `int 13h` error that
our read window turns into end of file. 1.44 MB drives are 80-cylinder and
have neither problem.

**Do not sweep with the interrupt monitor.** It stops the guest on every
`INT 21h`, which is exactly right for diagnosis and hopeless for triage: Num
Munchers makes 31,301 of them. Boot, wait a fixed number of GUEST seconds,
screenshot, read `Marty.disk()` — the drive counters are what separate *slow*
from *hung*, and they cost nothing.
