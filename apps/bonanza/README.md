# Bounce Bonanza for os8088

A native 8086 rolling-ball game: a ball you roll directly over an isometric
course that **hardware-scrolls in both directions** on the two 1983 graphics
cards, the CGA (320x200, four colours) and the Hercules (a 360x200 field of
pair pixels). It is this project's own design, not a remake: the ball is
generated by `tools/bonanza_assets.py` from code, the courses are text
(`apps/bonanza/courses/*.bbc`) compiled by `tools/bbcourse.py`, and nothing
else is read at build time or at run time. See SPEC.md 130 and
`docs/plans/BOUNCE-BONANZA-PLAN.md`.

**Status: wave 6, feel.** Twelve courses since 2026-11-25 ("Kite Hill", "Lodestone Gorge", "Clockwork Keep"
and "Castle Climb" joined the eight below; the map's order is SPEC.md 130.22.11) - two called "First Roll" (FIRST
and ROLL), "Little Hop", "Downhill Decision" (which took "Bounce Trial"'s
place on 2026-11-21; TRIAL is a test course now), "Windmill Lane" (its new
design, a fork, a turntable and a see-saw, in the prototype's place since the
same day), "Moonlit Green",
"Summit Rush" and the "Playground" - with flags, ball-swap pads, springs, tubes,
windmills, bumper posts, a lift and the cup; three balls (rubber, steel,
glass), each with its own Hop and Grip, and rubber's one BOOST (Space again
at a Hop's first contact); best
times kept on disk; demos saved and loaded on
disk (W, L), benchmarked (D) and shipped (V); the mouse as a tilt stick (T);
and music and impacts (S). There is no in-play HUD (it did not fit; SPEC.md
130.14.16) - the time is on the results face. SPEC.md 130.10 - 130.14 and
130.20 are the contract.

**The test build.** The keys below - and the benchmark, the results page
and the demo keys - are the TEST INSTRUMENTS, and since wave 4
of the space plan they exist only in a `make BUILD=build/bzdev BZDEV=1
bonanzadisk` build (SPEC.md 130.26.6.1). The shipped game's window answers
Enter, F and Alt+Enter (Play Game), F5 and F6 (save and load a replay) and
its menus. The camera is LATTICE alone: TRICKLE, which C used to select, is
deleted (SPEC.md 130.9).

Keys, on the desktop: Enter or Alt+Enter plays the game; **F plays the
selected course** - "First Roll", or one a test pokes into `bz_crsel` - with
that course's own ball (there is no key to pick a course or a ball: N, M and
the tour G went on 2026-11-19, SPEC.md 130.26.8, because rows that pressed
them depended on the courses' order). **B runs the benchmark** on its own
course, "Proving Ground" (in the test build alone: the shipped game does not
carry it), with that course's ball (the autopilot drives the
course's route for 1,200 frames, then the window shows the frames, the
display rate, the refreshes missed and the worst frame, and the cost of each
part of a frame). A key held down counts as ONE press. **R replays the last
play** - the same steps, on either card - once one has been recorded
(SPEC.md 130.11.12). Any key after a benchmark's results returns the window
to its main face. Playing: the arrows roll the ball, Space hops, Left Shift
grips; Esc, F or Alt+Enter goes back to the desktop. While playing, **P
pauses** and Backspace returns the ball to its last flag. The best course
times are kept in `SYSTEM\APPDATA\BONANZA.DAT` on the game's own disk.
After any play or replay the face shows its time, how it ended, its falls
and the flags it reached.

**Palettes** (SPEC.md 130.16.6): a course picks the CGA's palette 1 (cyan,
magenta, white; the default), palette 0 (green, red, brown) or palette 2
(cyan, red, white, mode 5's), bright or dim, over any ground colour - the
course builder's "CGA palette" on its course tab, or a `palette` line. The
VGA shows the same colours; the Hercules draws by pattern and has none.

**The Playground** (SPEC.md 130.16.8) is the balls' testing ground, in the
CGA's other palette: behind its apron a
STEP LADDER of four terraces - half a unit (every ball rolls up), one unit
(rubber's and glass's Hop), a unit and a half (rubber's Hop and its BOOST)
and two units (nobody's) - each a dead end; a GAP LADDER of one, two and
three tiles cut into the apron's front edge; a BRAKE STRIP ending at a deep
pit, with ice and sand lanes beside its run-up; a bounce mat; a pad for each
ball; and a goal at the end of a rolling route. Its demo (the sixth V) shows
the Hop, the boost onto the ledge, Grip on the strip and a pad.

**Pickups** (SPEC.md 130.25.9, 130.55): an hourglass (+10 s on the clock,
once an attempt), a SPEED and a JUMP that last 10 s by default (a course may
set its own `for`), the ball sparkling while one runs and blinking in its last
second; a course can mark one `respawn`, so it comes back when it wears off.
A HEART gives a life (up to 9) in the game, the ball's sparkles rising for a
second; a course's heart is back at each entry, or with `once` given once a
game. It is hidden in EXPLORE, free plays, replays and demos.

**Demos** (SPEC.md 130.14.7): **W saves** the last play (or the demo held) as
a `.BZR` file through the Save dialog, and **L loads** one through the Open
dialog; a `.BZR` is BONANZA's document, so double-clicking one in a Disk
window opens the game with it. A loaded file is checked in full before it is
used - another build's course, other physics, a damaged or foreign file is
refused and the demo held is kept. **V** takes the next of the six shipped
demos (one a course), **R** watches the demo held and **D** times it:
the demo replayed with the benchmark's instruments, its results on the same
page as B's. **T** switches the control between KEYS and MOUSE: with the
mouse the pointer is a tilt stick - its offset from where it was when the
play started (or the ball was last put back) tilts the course that way, a
quarter of the screen being a full tilt - the left button hops and the right
grips; a mouse play's demo records the tilt, so it replays without one.
There is no clock on the glass: the course time, the falls and the flags
reached are on the face after every play (SPEC.md 130.14.16). An on-screen
clock was built and measured, cost the CGA 65 missed frames on `MILL` with
the speaker on, and was taken out (SPEC.md 130.14.5).

**Sound** (SPEC.md 130.20): a title theme plays while the window is open and
each course has its own (the first five planned: *Fairway*, *Bounce Trial*,
*Windmill Lane*, *Moonlit Green*, *Summit Rush*), on the PC speaker or, with
`SOUND.DRV` and an AdLib, on the OPL2. Every impact - a landing, a bounce, a
wall, a bumper, a windmill's knock - sounds different for each ball: rubber
boings, steel clanks, glass tinks (and shatters). **S cycles the sound**:
AUTO (the AdLib where the Control Panel's route allows it, else the speaker),
SPEAKER, EFFECTS ONLY and OFF; the face names the choice and the arm found.
The songs are text (`apps/bonanza/music/*.bmu`) packed by `tools/bbmusic.py`,
which also renders them to WAV on the host:
`python3 tools/bbmusic.py wav -o DIR`.

Needs a CGA, a Hercules or a VGA card (the VGA in mode 0Dh, SPEC.md 130.46;
an EGA or an MDA is refused with the reason) and kern_big or kern_mega; the 128KB machine's kernel refuses it.

Build:

```
make bonanza        # build/bonanza.o88
make bonanzadisk    # build/bonanza.img, bonanza720.img, bonanza120.img, bonanza360.img
make bonanza-check  # the ball generator's own gate
make bonanza-course-check  # the course compiler's own gate
make bonanza-music-check   # the music packer's own gate
```
