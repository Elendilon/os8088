# Bounce Bonanza for os8088

A native 8086 rolling-ball game: a ball you roll directly over an isometric
course that **hardware-scrolls in both directions** on the two 1983 graphics
cards, the CGA (320x200, four colours) and the Hercules (a 360x200 field of
pair pixels). It is this project's own design, not a remake: the ball is
generated by `tools/bonanza_assets.py` from code, the courses are text
(`apps/bonanza/courses/*.bbc`) compiled by `tools/bbcourse.py`, and nothing
else is read at build time or at run time. See SPEC.md 128 and
`docs/plans/BOUNCE-BONANZA-PLAN.md`.

**Status: wave 1, the course pipeline and physics v1.** Two text courses,
"First Roll" and the benchmark's "Proving Ground", compiled into parts that
carry the picture, the occluders the ball rolls behind and the physics grid;
the 64-frame phase-torus ball. Physics v1 (SPEC.md 128.10.7): slopes pull
the ball, walls stop it and it slides along them, it flies off a lip and
lands, a pit puts it back on the last safe tile, and the goal ends the play
with its time on the face. There is no bounce, sound or scoring yet.

Keys, on the desktop: Enter, F or Alt+Enter plays the small course "First
Roll"; **B runs the benchmark** on its own course, "Proving Ground" (the
autopilot drives the course's route for 1,200 frames, then the window shows
the frames, the display rate, the refreshes missed and the worst frame, and
the cost of each part of a frame; the benchmark always runs the GLIDE
camera). **C cycles the camera mode** - GLIDE, FOLLOW, TRICKLE (SPEC.md
128.9) - on the desktop, where the face names it, and while playing; the
choice lasts until the package closes. Playing: the arrows roll the ball;
Esc, F or Alt+Enter goes back to the desktop.

Needs a CGA or a Hercules card (anything else is refused with the reason)
and kern_big or kern_mega; the 128KB machine's kernel refuses it.

Build:

```
make bonanza        # build/bonanza.o88
make bonanzadisk    # build/bonanza.img, bonanza720.img, bonanza120.img, bonanza360.img
make bonanza-check  # the ball generator's own gate
make bonanza-course-check  # the course compiler's own gate
```
