# apps/tithe/cards.txt - TITHE's CARD TABLE, the ONE source (SPEC.md 97.11)

# tools/os88tithecards.py reads it for the package (apps/tithe/ticards.inc)
# and tools/duelsim.py for the simulator, so the two cannot drift. It is a
# FIRST DRAFT (TITHE-PLAN 7.1, wave 2): every number here is a guess that the
# wave-11 harness exists to replace, and the shapes are what is binding -
# TITHE-PLAN 7.1's thirty slots, three commanders a faction, at most eight
# pure specialists.
#
# A CHARACTER (role melee, ranged, shield, gen, ident, cmdr):
#   name | role | cost | POWER | FRONT m r hp s g h KEYWORDS | REAR m r hp s g h KEYWORDS
# An ORDER (role order) has one block, its EFFECT for this round:
#   name | order | cost | - | +m +r +s +g +h KEYWORDS
#
# cost is gold and souls: 3g, 2s, 5g1s, and a card's COST TIER (TITHE-PLAN
# 7.1's template) is the two added. m r hp s g h is MELEE, RANGED, HP,
# SHIELD, GOLD, HEAL (TITHE-PLAN 5.2). A keyword with a number is written
# BULWARK1. The keywords, and what each does, are SPEC.md 97.11.3.
#
# A STARTER DECK (TITHE-PLAN 9.1) is `deck FACTION: name*copies, name, ...`.
#
# A NAME IS AT MOST 12 CHARACTERS: capitals, digits, space and - . + / , :
# (the faces carry nothing else - tools/os88titheface.py).
# =============================================================================
