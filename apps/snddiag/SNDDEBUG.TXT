os8088 SOUND DEBUG DISK
=======================

This disk boots os8088 and opens Sound Debug by itself. It is for when
sound does not work the way it should on your machine - a card that is not
found, or one that plays for a moment and then goes quiet.

Its SOUND.DRV is built from the same source as the one on the system disk,
with a record kept of everything it probes and everything a stream does.
Sound Debug shows that record on five pages:

  1  DETECT     where the card answered, its DSP version, how its IRQ was
                found, the mixer, an ESS chip's own identity, OPL, MPU-401,
                and a BLASTER= line
  2  PLAY       a tone for ten seconds through the same path music players
                use, one line a second: bytes played, block interrupts, the
                DSP's own voice switch, the mixer, the DMA controller's
                count, the stream's state
  3  REGS       the mixer's registers, the last bytes sent to the DSP, the
                stream record and the interrupt controller
  4  LAB        the DMA lab: sixteen short runs, each driving the card and
  5  MORE       its DMA channel a different way for 2.4 seconds, and what
                the DMA controller did in each

It runs everything by itself and SAVES ITS OWN REPORT to this disk as text
files. Leave the disk in the drive and not write-protected.

WHAT TO SEND US
---------------
1. Boot this disk and wait. It takes about a minute and a half:
   page 1, then the ten-second tone (page 2), then the lab (page 4),
   whose runs each play the tone again for 2.4 seconds.
2. Listen. Note whether the ten-second tone played all the way through,
   and which lab runs (the number shows at the top right of page 4)
   played their whole 2.4 seconds and which went quiet early.
3. When page 4 says Done, the reports are on the disk:
   SOUNDA1.TXT (written after the tone) and SOUNDB1.TXT (after the lab).
   A second boot writes SOUNDA2.TXT and SOUNDB2.TXT, and so on - nothing
   is overwritten.
4. Copy the SOUND*.TXT files off the disk on any PC (it is an ordinary
   FAT floppy) and send them, with what you heard and the card's name.

Keys: 1 2 3 pages, P runs the play test again, R changes its rate (22050,
11025, 8000, 44100 Hz), V turns the D8h voice reading off and on, Esc quits.

If the tone stops when D8h sampling is on and plays through when it is off,
say so: that is a finding too.
